01 · What I found

A sharp product with a few fixable funnel leaks.

In shortKayaan has real advantages. A few site claims and one generic calendar link are likely costing pilots.

Working for you

  • Operator founders. The CEO is a second-generation fleet owner who still runs trucks.
  • A driver app in drivers' hands (KAY, live since Aug 2025). Most broker tools have no driver relationship.
  • Per-load pricing, no seats, live in 48 hours, a named TMS list.
  • The site already leads with Montgomery and the audit trail.

Worth fixing in week one

  • Four stories on two sites (voice booking, Shield, Autopilot, autonomous brokerage). Lead with Shield.
  • One calendar link for every button, with 15, 20 or 30 minute demos depending on the page. Add an intake form.
  • No case study or price range yet, so buyers can't sanity-check before booking.
  • SOC 2 wording differs between pages ("in progress" vs "compliance").

04 · Three plays

One to lead with, one in parallel, one to build.

In shortLead with a free carrier readout that shows value before any demo. Add insurance agents as a warm channel. Build a driver network loop later.

LeadThe Reasonable Care Readout

The Montgomery case turned on a carrier with a conditional safety rating, which anyone can check in public data. Most brokers have never looked at their recent carriers that way.

Who
Owner or VP Ops at 10 to 75 person brokers, picked by the engine.
The offer
Send me 10 carriers you used last week. In 24 hours you get a one-page check: safety ratings, authority age, insurance on file, recent contact changes.
Then
A 20-minute demo on their own carriers, then Shield on their next 20 loads with success goals signed up front.
Success signal
3%+ positive replies, 8+ readouts requested in the first month.
Risk and fix
Brokers may not share carrier lists with a stranger. Fallback: a readout on carriers posting lanes into their city, plus agent-introduced readouts.
ParallelRenewal Desk

Since the ruling, insurers are asking brokers how they vet carriers before they renew. Agents need clients who look insurable.

Who
Transportation insurance agents with broker clients.
The offer
A free carrier-selection review for their clients before renewal. The 20-load Reasonable Care File goes into the renewal.
Why it converts
Warm introductions with a deadline attached.
Success signal
1+ agent partner and 3+ introductions by day 45.
Risk and fix
Agents may avoid recommending vendors. Position it as an educational review, with no referral fees.
BuildVerified Once

Drivers hate repeating ID checks for every broker. Kayaan is rare in having both a driver app and a broker product.

How it works
A driver verifies once (with consent) and reuses it on any Kayaan-protected load. They can send "I'm Kayaan Verified" to other brokers, which brings those brokers in.
Success signal
3+ broker demo requests from 10 participating carriers.
Risk and fix
Biometric privacy rules. Counsel review first, explicit opt-in, minimal data stored.

Not recommended now: paid ads, generic content, or cold AI voice calls (legal risk and the wrong first impression for a fraud-prevention brand).
