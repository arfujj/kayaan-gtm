04 · Three plays

One to lead with, one in parallel, one to build.

  1. LeadThe Reasonable Care Readout"Send me 10 carriers you used last week. In 24 hours you get a one-page check: safety ratings, authority age, insurance, recent contact changes." Then: run Shield on your next 20 loads.
  2. ParallelRenewal DeskInsurance agents now need insurable broker clients. They introduce brokers before renewal, and the 20-load file goes into the renewal.
  3. BuildVerified OnceA driver verified once can reuse it on any Kayaan load, and can send "I'm Kayaan Verified" to other brokers, bringing them in.

05 · The engine

Public data in, qualified pilots out. A person approves every first message.

25,081brokers from FMCSA→ buying signals→ enrich + verify→ score 0 to 100→ AI drafts→ human approves→ send→ sort replies→ qualify call→ learn weekly

Signals: hiring dispatch staff, theft or impersonation news, insurance renewal, visits to the pilot page. Every score shows its reasons. No scraping, no cold AI calls. A working scoring prototype is already built.
