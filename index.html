The idea

Since the Supreme Court's Montgomery ruling in May 2026, a freight broker can be sued for picking an unsafe carrier. Shield already records, on every load, that the broker checked the carrier, the driver and the truck. I'd sell that record as proof of care to brokers with 10 to 75 people, who feel this the most and have nobody doing compliance.

What I'd do for you

  1. MILE1

    Find the right brokers

    Build the list from the government's public broker database (25,081 active brokers today), then pick out the ones hiring dispatch staff, hit by theft, or close to an insurance renewal.

  2. MILE2

    Get them to answer

    Offer something useful first: Send me 10 carriers you used last week and I'll send back a free safety check within 24 hours. Then invite them to run Shield on 20 loads.

  3. MILE3

    Turn pilots into customers

    Agree on what success means before each pilot starts. Every pilot ends with a short report the broker can hand to their insurance agent.

  4. MILE4

    Add a referral channel

    Insurance agents now need their broker clients to look safe. They can send brokers to Kayaan before renewal.

In week one: tighten the website to one clear message, swap two claims a freight veteran would doubt, and add a short intake form so demo time goes to real prospects.
