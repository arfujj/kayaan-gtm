The idea

Sell the proof of care to the brokers who feel the ruling most.

Since Montgomery, a broker can be sued for picking an unsafe carrier. Shield records, on every load, that the broker checked the carrier, the driver and the truck. That record is the product for brokerages with 10 to 75 people and nobody doing compliance.

9 to 0, May 14, 2026Negligent-selection claims against brokers are no longer preempted.
Insurers are tighteningUnderwriters now ask brokers how they vet carriers before they renew.
Fraud moved after the rate conHighway's 2026 reports tie about 40% of stolen loads to compromised communications.
What I'd do for you

Four steps from a public list to a paying broker.

STEP 01

Find the right brokers

Build the list from FMCSA's public broker data, then pick out the ones hiring dispatch staff, hit by theft, or close to an insurance renewal.

STEP 02

Get them to answer

Lead with something useful: Send me 10 carriers you used last week and I'll send back a free safety check in 24 hours. Then offer Shield on 20 loads.

STEP 03

Turn pilots into ARR

Agree on success goals before each pilot. Each pilot ends with a short Reasonable Care File the broker can hand to their insurance agent.

STEP 04

Add a referral channel

Insurance agents need their broker clients to look safe. They can send brokers to Kayaan before renewal.

Week one: sharpen the site to one message, swap two claims a freight veteran would doubt, and add a short intake form so demo time goes to real prospects.
