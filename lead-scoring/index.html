Which brokers to call first, and why.

This tool pulls active freight brokers from FMCSA's public data, scores each one out of 100 on fit and buying intent, and explains every point. It's the first step of the engine in the plan.

View the code on GitHub → lead_scoring.py
How it works
25,081active brokers (FMCSA)→ rule out bad fits→ fit score /50→ intent score /50→ buyer + opening angle

Public, keyless government data. No scraping. Hiring, theft and renewal signals are inputs from licensed or partner sources.
